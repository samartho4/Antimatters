# Vision: Information Physics

**"Information is physical." — Rolf Landauer**

For decades, science has treated *Information*, *Matter*, and *Energy* as distinct domains.
*   **Information Science**: Bits, Logic, Code.
*   **Material/Life Science**: Atoms, Cells, Physics.

Antimatters is built on the realization that this distinction is artificial. In the modern era, **Information is equivalent to Energy and Matter**. Landauer's Principle taught us that manipulating information has a physical energy cost ($kT \ln 2$). Conversely, physical systems (like proteins) are merely condensed information processing engines.

## Expanding the Search Space

We believe that the next great leap in discovery will come from merging these domains. By integrating **Information Science** (LLMs, Knowledge Graphs) directly with **Material Science** (Molecular Dynamics, Docking), we expand the search space of what is discoverable.

We are not just building "better tools." We are building a system where:
1.  **Literature is Data**: A paper on PubMed is treated with the same rigour as a crystal structure in the PDB.
2.  **Insight has Mass**: A validated Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR) is a physical asset that reduces the energy required for the next discovery.

## Case Study: Intrinsically Disordered Proteins (IDPs)

To prove this thesis, we are tackling one of the hardest problems in biology: **Intrinsically Disordered Proteins (IDPs)**.

IDPs are the "dark matter" of the proteome. They have no single fixed structure, defying the traditional "lock-and-key" paradigm of drug discovery. They exist as dynamic *ensembles* of conformations—a problem that requires both massive **Information** (context about their behavior) and massive **Computation** (simulating their physics).

Antimatters treats IDP Ligand Docking not as the *end goal*, but as the **perfect workflow** to demonstrate our vision:
*   **Information**: We use Agents to mine the literature (`BioContext`) for likely binding sites.
*   **Matter**: We check the structural reality (`PED`) of the protein ensemble.
*   **Energy**: We simulate the physical binding (`Vina`) across parallel conformations.

This is **Information Physics** in action: using bits to navigate atoms, turning compute into cure.
