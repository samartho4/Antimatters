# Antimatters

### A Multi-Agent Platform for Scientific Acceleration

Science is currently fragmented. We run experiments in silos, analyze results in isolation, and lose the vital context that connects a hypothesis to its conclusion. Antimatters is built to solve this by treating the entire scientific workflow—from literature synthesis to molecular docking and insight generation—as a unified, agentic process.

We are starting with **Generative Material Science & Life Sciences**, specifically focusing on the complex problem of **Intrinsically Disordered Proteins (IDPs)**. But our vision is broader: to realize the convergence of Information, Energy, and Matter.

## The Problem: Fragmented Discovery

In traditional workflows, a "negative result" is often a dead end. In Antimatters, every result—positive or negative—feeds into a persistent knowledge graph.
*   **Information Silos**: Tools for literature (PubMed) don't talk to tools for simulation (AutoDock).
*   **Lost Insights**: Structure-Activity Relationships (SAR) found in one experiment rarely inform the next automatically.
*   **Cognitive Load**: Scientists spend more time formatting data than interpreting physics.

## The Solution: Agents That Remember

Antimatters deploys a squad of specialized agents—Researcher, Engineer, Evolution—orchestrated by the **Vibe Framework**.
*   **Gemini 3** Reasoning Engine: We don't just "generate" text; we use 16k thinking budgets to reason through complex SAR patterns.
*   **First Principles**: Our agents understand physics. They don't just match strings; they analyze π-stacking, hydrophobic pockets, and energetic stability.
*   **Continuous Evolution**: The system builds a MedGraphRAG-structured knowledge graph that grows smarter with every experiment.
